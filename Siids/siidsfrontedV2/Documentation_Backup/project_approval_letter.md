Application Unit (RRA) hereby acknowledges receipt and review of the academic project proposal submitted by **[Contributor Name]**, a final year student, entitled **“Digitalization of Physical Stock Operations for Seized Goods,”** and confirms that they are permitted to proceed with the project in line with Rwanda Revenue Authority requirements. 

The project aims to address the challenges of tracking seized goods and physical evidence by developing a dedicated digital Stock Operations System. This platform will streamline the management of seized goods by establishing a clear workflow between Temporary Storage (managed by Surveillance Officers) and Main Storage (overseen by Stock Managers). By digitalizing these critical logistics, the system ensures a transparent and organized chain of custody. Furthermore, it is anticipated that the data structure and workflows developed in this project will later be integrated with the broader Investigation framework, allowing Investigation Officers to directly send and track seized goods through the same centralized pipeline.
